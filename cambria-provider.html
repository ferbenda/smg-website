Cambria Quartz Surfaces

Cambria is a family-owned, American-made producer of premium engineered quartz surfaces. Based in Le Sueur, Minnesota, Cambria crafts every surface from pure natural quartz — one of the hardest minerals on earth — delivering unmatched durability, zero maintenance, and designs that rival the beauty of natural stone.

Specialty

Cambria specializes in engineered quartz surfaces composed of approximately 93% pure natural quartz with resin binders and pigments. Their product line spans countertops, backsplashes, shower walls, fireplace surrounds, and vanities — available in over 200 unique designs ranging from classic whites and dramatic veined marbles to bold blacks and warm earth tones.

Unlike natural stone, Cambria quartz is nonporous and never requires sealing. It resists stains, scratches, and bacterial growth, making it an ideal choice for high-use kitchens and bathrooms in Colorado's active households.
