Who We Are

Where Mountain Living Meets Craftsmanship

Founded in 2019 in the heart of the Yampa Valley, Steamboat Masters Granite has grown into Northwest Colorado's most trusted name in premium stone surfaces. We bring over 25 years of combined expertise to every project — studying each slab, planning every cut for maximum beauty, and treating every home as our own.

We are not just fabricators. We are consultants who guide you through material selection, edge profiles, and layout planning before a single cut is made.

Uncompromising Quality

Every slab hand-inspected, every edge precision-finished, every installation meticulously executed.

Client-First Approach

We listen, guide, and adapt to your vision, lifestyle, and budget — every step of the way.

Speed Without Sacrifice

Local fabrication means faster turnaround. Some projects go from slab to installation within the same week.

100-Mile Coverage

Serving Steamboat Springs and a 100-mile radius — fully insured with a lifetime guarantee on every installation.
