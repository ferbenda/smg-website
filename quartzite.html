Why Quartzite

Extreme Heat Resistance

One of the most heat-resistant natural stones available. Hot pans are not a concern.

Harder Than Granite

Ranks 7 on the Mohs scale. Extremely scratch resistant and durable for any application.

Natural Movement

Dramatic veining and translucent qualities that rival marble at a fraction of the maintenance.

UV Resistant

Will not fade or discolor from sunlight exposure. Perfect for outdoor kitchens and sun-filled rooms.

Exotic Beauty Meets Strength

Quartzite is a metamorphic rock formed when sandstone is subjected to extreme heat and pressure deep within the earth. The result is a stone harder than granite with the fluid, marble-like veining that designers and homeowners love.

Popular varieties like Taj Mahal, Super White, Calacatta Macaubas, and Blue Louise showcase the extraordinary range of colors and patterns available — from soft whites and creams to dramatic blues and greens.

Because quartzite is a natural stone, every slab is unique. We carefully inspect and hand-select slabs to ensure you get the most stunning piece for your project.

Quartzite vs Quartz — Know the Difference

Despite the similar names, quartzite and quartz are fundamentally different. Quartzite is 100% natural stone, mined from quarries. Quartz is an engineered product made from crushed stone mixed with resins.

Quartzite offers the natural beauty and uniqueness that cannot be replicated by any engineered material, along with superior heat resistance and UV stability.

Like granite, quartzite benefits from periodic sealing — a simple process we handle during installation and teach you to maintain annually.
