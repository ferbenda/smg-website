Francini Natural Stone

Francini Inc. is a leading natural stone distributor with a focus on premium granite, marble, and quartzite slabs. With decades of experience in the stone industry, Francini maintains direct relationships with quarries across Brazil, Italy, India, and other key stone-producing regions to deliver exceptional materials to fabricators, designers, and homeowners.

Specialty

Francini specializes in natural stone slab distribution: granite in hundreds of colors and patterns, marble from classic Carrara to dramatic Calacatta, quartzite in both polished and leathered finishes, and specialty materials for unique design applications. Their inventory is constantly refreshed with new arrivals from global quarries.

Francini's strength is their quality control — every slab is evaluated for structural integrity, color consistency, and surface finish before entering inventory. This ensures fabricators and homeowners receive stone that meets the highest standards for both beauty and performance.

Why Buy Through Steamboat Masters Granite

Francini gives us access to a constantly rotating inventory of premium natural stone that keeps our offerings fresh and competitive. When a client wants a specific granite, marble, or quartzite, Francini's deep stock and direct quarry access means we can find it — often faster and at better pricing than going through larger distributors.

We pair Francini's exceptional stone with our precision CNC fabrication and professional installation, delivering a seamless experience from slab selection to final walk-through.
