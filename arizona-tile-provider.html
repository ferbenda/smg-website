Arizona Tile Stone & Surfaces

Founded in 1977 by Heisman Trophy winner John Huarte, Arizona Tile has grown into one of the largest independently owned importers of stone, quartz, and tile in the United States. With locations across 10 western states and direct quarry relationships worldwide, they offer over 300 varieties of natural stone and 65+ colors of Della Terra Quartz.

Specialty

Arizona Tile distributes an extraordinary range of surfaces: natural stone slabs (granite, marble, quartzite, limestone, travertine), their proprietary Della Terra Quartz line, Della Terra Porcelain slabs, and an extensive catalog of porcelain, ceramic, and glass tile for floors, walls, and backsplashes.

Their natural stone is hand-selected from quarries in Brazil, Italy, Spain, and India — each slab inspected for quality before reaching their distribution network. The Della Terra Quartz line blends nature and technology for easy-care, color-consistent surfaces at accessible price points.

Why Buy Through Steamboat Masters Granite

Through our relationship with Arizona Tile, we access their vast inventory of natural stone and quartz slabs at competitive wholesale pricing. This gives our Steamboat Springs clients access to materials typically reserved for major metro showrooms — without the markup.

Whether you want an exotic Brazilian quartzite, a classic Italian marble, or a durable Della Terra Quartz countertop, we source the perfect slab and handle everything from template to installation.
